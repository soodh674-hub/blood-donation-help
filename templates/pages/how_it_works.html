How BloodLife Works

A simple, transparent process connecting donors with those in need

1

Request Blood

Fill out our simple blood request form with patient details, hospital information, and upload a prescription for verification. Our system auto-fills your profile information to save time.

  • Auto-fill from your profile
  • Prescription upload required
  • Date validation prevents past dates
2

Admin Verification

Our admin team reviews your request and verifies the uploaded prescription. This ensures all requests are legitimate and helps maintain trust in our community.

⏱️ Typical verification time: 15-30 minutes during business hours

3

Broadcast to Donors

Once approved, your request is broadcast to nearby registered donors based on:

📍 Location Matching

Donors within your city/area receive notifications

🩸 Blood Group Match

Compatible blood group donors are prioritized

4

Donor Acceptance & Chat

When a donor accepts your request, you can communicate directly through our integrated chat system to coordinate pickup/delivery details.

  • Real-time messaging
  • Share location & ETA
5

Track in Real-Time

Monitor your request status through our Zomato-style tracking dashboard with live map updates.

Status Timeline:

Submitted → Verified → Searching → Matched → In Transit → Completed
6

Completion & Rating

After successful donation, both parties can rate each other to help build a trustworthy community of life-savers.
